Channel controls appear here after connecting to your ESP32.

All outputs start OFF. Pair frequency 0 disables both sides. At 0% duty the low side is continuously HIGH; at 100% the high side is continuously HIGH when enabled.

Pulse and burst outputs are 3.3 V logic. Analog waveforms use an 8-bit DAC, centered near half the supply voltage; amplitude is a percentage of its nominal full range. The raw DAC is not a calibrated or 50 Ω laboratory output.

Analog modes: requested 10–5000 Hz, rounded in about 9.77 Hz steps, at 40000 samples/second per channel. Waveforms become coarser toward 5000 Hz. Changing one analog channel briefly restarts any other active analog channel.

This page controls the ESP32 over USB serial or HTTP over TCP/IP. Closing or disconnecting leaves the last applied output state in place. Use Stop all outputs first if desired.
